OVERVIEW

Mobile Fox One is an elite anomalous extraction and rescue task force operating directly under Executive Command.


Its primary mission is the preservation, recovery, and emergency evacuation of designated A-Class Masterminds.


Unlike containment units, Mobile Fox One is authorized to abandon facilities, anomalies, assets, and infrastructure if such sacrifices are necessary to preserve an A-Class Mastermind.


The exact legal basis for this authority remains EXECUTIVE ORDER FOX-13.

HISTORY

MFO-1 was formed following Incident-00. The incident resulted in the death of a high-value A-Class Mastermind. Subsequent analysis concluded that the loss of a single mastermind caused more damage than the destruction of seventeen containment facilities.
